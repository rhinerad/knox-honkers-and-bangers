Who we are

Our mission

We're Knoxville's street band. We take loud, joyful brass and drums to where people already are, show up for our neighbors and the causes we believe in, and make music that anyone can join.

What we stand for

Four things we believe

Music belongs in the street

We play sidewalks, parks and parade routes. No tickets, no stage, no velvet rope.

Show up for neighbors

Pride festivals, a community bail fund, a workers' picnic. We bring the noise for causes that matter here.

Anyone can honk

Beginners, lapsed band kids and lifelong players march side by side. If you can keep a beat, there's a spot for you.

Joy is the point

We play to get people dancing, grinning and moving down the street together.
