What's honk?

Brass band music for the street

Honk is a street band movement. Horns and drums play loud and close, out among the people instead of up on a stage. The sound borrows from New Orleans second lines, Balkan brass, funk, punk, samba batucada and anything else that works at marching tempo.

Honk bands show up at parades, rallies, block parties and festivals all over the world. The Knoxville Honkers and Bangers bring it to East Tennessee.

  • Out in the openNo stage and no barrier. If you can hear us, you're part of the show.
  • Everybody movesThe band walks, the crowd follows, and somebody always ends up dancing next to the tuba.
  • Loud for good causesWe play Pride, benefits, picnics and parades for our neighbors.
